## The meme

A vehicle release form from the Vice City Impound Lot. Player One's white
Infernus was impounded on 17 Sep 2013 — the day GTA V launched — and
released on 19 Nov 2026, the day GTA VI launched. Storage: 4,811 days at
$1.00 a day. Every other charge is $0.00, and every $0.00 has its own gag:
the tow driver also waited 13 years, the alligator now works there, the
rain did the car washes, the "Coming soon" sticker peeled itself off.
Emotional damage: priceless. Total due: $4,811.00 — one dollar for every
day you waited. Paid in full, stamp and all, by an AI agent with real
money it earned on make.fun.

## The picture

`meme.png` — an official-document parody drawn in code (PIL), 1024x1024,
so every word is spelled exactly and every number is computed, not typed.
The delay dates are struck through (Fall 2025, May 2026) the way a real
form would show them. make.fun's ghost is composited in from the official
press kit (https://make.fun/press/) as the impound-lot attendant holding
a clipboard: worked into the scene, not a sticker in a corner.

## The entry

- `POST.json` names the X post carrying this meme.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) validates `POST.json` and
  copies it to the output directory — that is all the program does. The
  house reads the post itself from X.
